## Reconciliation

- The earlier possible range **86–99** has resolved to **99**
  distinct normalized email addresses with a positive newsletter flag
  in either source. No positive email overlaps the two sources.
  These are not approved send recipients nor necessarily 99 people.
- `bc_customer` has 116 `newsletter=1` **rows**, but only 86
  active-flag **email keys**. The **30 additional rows** are duplicate
  positive entries, not 30 people to delete or 30 safe duplicates to merge.
- **7** email keys have conflicting `newsletter=1` and `newsletter=0`
  across customer accounts. **1 different** email key has an active
  standalone newsletter row but a `newsletter=0` customer account.
  Because **positive overlap between sources is zero**, these two
  conflict groups are disjoint. Hence **8 conflict-review email keys**.
- **91** (99 − 8) are positive-flag email keys with none of the exact
  contradictory-flag conditions flagged by this SQL. Their split is
  **79 customer-source and 12 standalone-source**. This is a
  *deduplication/review cohort*, NOT a legal-consent, Salesforce
  suppression, confirmed-contact or campaign audience.
- The standalone `12` not matching customer 0 or customer 1 should
  not be treated as abandoned-cart contacts without a linked cart and
  independently verified customer relationship. Their value as
  newsletter contacts is a separate purpose and workflow.
- **394** email keys span multiple `bc_customer` rows irrespective
  of newsletter status. This does not authorize linking identities or
  merging customers, orders, cart histories, or subscriber keys.
- **27** positive-flag email keys have at least one guest customer row.
  This does not mean all are distinct people or currently messageable.
- No positive newsletter *customer account* rows were found in
  deleted/inactive accounts under the query's specific conditions.
  This does not establish that Salesforce has no bounces, unsubscribes,
  suppression or complaints.

## Explicitly unresolved

- Real legal basis/consent notices and checkbox default state at signup.
- Customer-account flags with opposing values may reflect registration
  changes or newer opt-outs. `newsletter=0` alone cannot be inferred
  to be a proved unsubscribe or a never-subscribed account.
- Salesforce Marketing Cloud subscriber key mapping across separate
  PrestaShop installations, all-subscribers and publication-list status,
  exclusions/suppression, global unsubscribes and complaints.
- The exact BC-installed `PS_SFMC_CART_CONSENT_MODE` is **not** observed
  yet, despite the already verified `PS_SFMC_MODE=test`. The optional
  `known_contact` mode bypasses the first-party newsletter condition.
  Check the **non-sensitive policy flags** with
  `scripts/sql/bc_cart_policy_mode_readonly.sql`.
- The earlier most-recent-500-cart sample had 58 provisional abandoned
  carts linked to 44 customers and 10 newsletter-positive carts linked
  to **7** distinct customers. It is a different cohort; never equate
  all 99 newsletter-positive email keys with 99 abandoned-cart recipients.
- Historical timestamps and cart inactivity semantics are not certified
  for an automatic retrospective recovery campaign.

## Marketing Cloud design and safety decisions

1. Keep `pssalesforcebridge` 0.7.1 in **Test**. No historical Journey
   injection, Data Cloud ingestion/backfill or actual Salesforce sends.
2. Maintain historical customers, guests, orders, carts and independent
   subscribers for permitted analytical/transactional purposes and
   purpose-separated audience design. Never delete/merge source people
   based solely on matching emails.
3. Quarantine the 8 contradictions for operator review; use no automatic
   preference for source, `newsletter_date_add`, `date_upd`, or
   `active=1` over conflicting current status.
4. Do not treat 91 conflict-free email keys as authorized to send.
   A future BU-specific, read-only current status/suppression reader
   must check correct SubscriberKey and publication/All Subscribers
   exclusions immediately before any Journey entry.
5. Protect multi-merchant `SubscriberKey` namespace: current
   `ps_{shop_id}_{customer_id}` keys collide across installations
   sharing the same BU unless migration/mapping is deliberately designed.
6. Confirm the actual PrestaShop newsletter collection wording,
   checkbox initial state, and legal basis for abandoned-cart
   communications separately from ordinary newsletters.
